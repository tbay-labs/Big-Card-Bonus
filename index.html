Good to know

Can I add two smaller cards together?

No. The bonus needs one single card with a payout of 500 points or more. Two cards of 300 and 250 points don't count, but any one card of 500+ does.

How many times can I get it?

Once every campaign week, every week: up to 5 times between 25 Nov and 31 Dec 2026. That's up to ₦25,000.

When is the ₦5,000 paid?

On the Thursday after each week closes (weeks run Wednesday to Tuesday). The last week runs 23–31 Dec and is paid on Thursday 7 Jan.

Which cards count?

All gift cards, in all currencies, as long as that one card's payout is 500 points or more.

Is it points or cash?

₦5,000 cash, added to your Tbay wallet.

Do I need to claim it?

No. It's added to your wallet automatically on payout day.

What if my trade is pending or disputed?

Only successful trades count. Disputed and rejected orders don't qualify, and a pending trade counts once it completes successfully within the week.
